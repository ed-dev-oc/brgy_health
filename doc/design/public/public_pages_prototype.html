ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About BrgyHealth

Bridging grassroots healthcare and modern digital convenience for every Filipino barangay.

Our Mission

In the Philippines, the Barangay Health Center (BHC) serves as the front line of primary care—delivering maternal check-ups, childhood immunizations, blood pressure monitoring, and health education. Yet most centers still rely on manual paper logbooks and physical bulletin boards.

BrgyHealth empowers any barangay to launch a dedicated, verified healthcare portal in minutes, giving residents transparent access to service schedules while equipping Barangay Health Workers with modern clinical queue and record management tools.

Community-Centered
Organized by Purok and Zone for accurate local outreach.
Multi-Channel Alerts
In-app notifications, WebPush, and email countdown reminders.
Tenant-Isolated
Each barangay operates securely on its own verified subdomain.

LEGAL & DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

How we safeguard resident medical records and govern platform usage.

Data Privacy & Patient Confidentiality

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), all patient personal information, appointment bookings, and clinical consultation notes are strictly scoped to your verified Barangay Health Center tenant.

1. Information We Collect

Full name, Purok/Zone subdivision, email address, phone number, and consultation history recorded by authorized Barangay Health Workers.

2. How Information Is Used

Used exclusively for scheduling barangay health services, sending appointment reminders, and generating anonymized or official clinic attendance reports.

Platform Terms of Use

By registering a Barangay Health Center or resident account on BrgyHealth, you agree to provide accurate identity and location details. Barangay Administrator accounts require SuperAdmin verification prior to public activation.
